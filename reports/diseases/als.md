## Limitations

Largest disease-specific multi-ancestry GWAS with summary statistics and Open Targets credible sets. L2G scores prioritize candidate genes but do not
prove causality. AHBA represents healthy adult donor expression and is neither a
patient map nor a diagnostic or predictive model.
