## Limitations

Dedicated contemporary epilepsy meta-analysis; broad biobank/cross-population atlas records were not preferred. L2G scores prioritize candidate genes but do not
prove causality. AHBA represents healthy adult donor expression and is neither a
patient map nor a diagnostic or predictive model.
