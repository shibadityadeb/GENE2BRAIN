## Limitations

Largest dedicated migraine GWAS with extensive Open Targets credible sets; cross-trait and PheCode records excluded. L2G scores prioritize candidate genes but do not
prove causality. AHBA represents healthy adult donor expression and is neither a
patient map nor a diagnostic or predictive model.
