## Limitations

Large dedicated PGC disease GWAS with 222 Open Targets credible sets; generic cross-population atlas studies were not preferred. L2G scores prioritize candidate genes but do not
prove causality. AHBA represents healthy adult donor expression and is neither a
patient map nor a diagnostic or predictive model.
